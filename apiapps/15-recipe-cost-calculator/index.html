Recipe builder

Enter any recipe with ingredient quantities and units from your prep notes. Mise handles the unit conversion math — mix grams, ounces, liters, and cups in the same recipe without rounding errors.

Supplier price sync

Enter your local supplier prices once and Mise applies them across every recipe that uses that ingredient. When your duck breast cost goes up on Tuesday's invoice, you'll see the margin impact across your whole menu immediately.

Food cost percentage

Mise calculates your food cost as a percentage of your menu price and flags dishes outside the 28–32% target range. You set the target; the calculator does the watching.

Recommended pricing

Set your target food cost percentage and Mise works backwards to the minimum menu price that protects your margin. A starting point, not a mandate — your market and format still govern the final number.

Margin health library

Your recipe library shows a green, amber, or red health indicator beside each dish. One glance tells you which plates are carrying the menu and which ones are quietly draining it.
