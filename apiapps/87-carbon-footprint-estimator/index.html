🌍 Methodology

Emissions = Distance (km) × Weight (tonnes) × Emission Factor (kg CO₂e / t-km)

All factors use Well-to-Wheel (WTW) boundaries, including upstream fuel extraction, refining, and transport. Factors for sea freight assume average vessel utilisation of 70%. Air factors include Radiative Forcing Index (RFI = 1.9) multiplier for high-altitude climate effects. Values are representative averages — actual emissions may vary ±20% by vehicle specification, load factor, route, and driving conditions.
