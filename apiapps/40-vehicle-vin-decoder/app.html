WMI (1–3)
World Manufacturer Identifier Country of origin, manufacturer, and vehicle division.
VDS (4–9)
Vehicle Descriptor Section Body style, engine type, restraints. Position 9 is the check digit.
VIS (10–17)
Vehicle Identifier Section Position 10 = model year, 11 = assembly plant, 12–17 = serial number.
