How k-Anonymity Works

1
Hash locally

Your password is hashed with SHA-1 entirely in your browser. It never leaves your device.

2
Send 5 chars only

Only the first 5 characters of the hash (out of 40) are sent to the HIBP API.

3
Receive matching hashes

The API returns all hashes starting with those 5 chars — hundreds of results to mask yours.

4
Compare locally

Your browser checks if your full hash appears in the list. The server never knows which password you checked.
