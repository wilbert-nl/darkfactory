What Goldenhour does

Everything a field photographer needs

🌅

Visual day timeline

A full 24-hour color-coded bar shows night, nautical twilight, civil twilight, golden hour, and solar noon at a glance — no squinting at numbers.

📅

7-day calendar view

Fetch sunrise and sunset for the next seven days in one tap. Compare day lengths to find your ideal shooting window before you even leave the house.

💾

Session planner

Save up to 20 shoot sessions — location, date, sun times — locally in your browser. Reload any session instantly with one click.

📍

Any location on earth

Search by city name with Nominatim autocomplete, or tap "My Location" to pull coordinates automatically. Accurate to the minute for any latitude.

🗓️

ICS calendar export

Download a .ics file with golden hour events for your session — import directly into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook.

🌙

Blue hour included

Civil twilight, nautical twilight, and blue hour are all calculated and shown — the moody cinematic window that many photographers prize as much as golden hour.
