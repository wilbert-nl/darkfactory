# MISSION.md — calendr

## What This Builds

An appointment scheduling platform with brandable booking pages, a service catalog, two-way Google and Outlook calendar sync, and automated WhatsApp/SMS reminders via Twilio. AI-powered post-appointment follow-up messages are generated by Claude (Haiku) on the backend. Clients can book directly from a public-facing branded page; providers manage their availability and services from a dashboard.

## Primary Users

- **Providers:** Freelancers, consultants, coaches, therapists, and small service businesses who need a bookable page and automated reminders.
- **Clients:** End customers booking appointments via a provider's public booking page (no account required to book).

## In Scope

- Provider accounts with dashboard
- Booking page with custom branding (logo, colors, headline)
- Service catalog: name, description, duration, price, buffer time (max 4 hours hardcoded)
- Two-way Google Calendar sync (google-auth-library + webhook + polling fallback)
- Two-way Microsoft Outlook calendar sync (Microsoft Graph API + subscription webhook)
- Availability rules (working hours, blocked dates, booking lead time)
- WhatsApp and SMS reminders via Twilio (booking confirmation, 24h reminder, day-of reminder)
- Reminder opt-out that always works regardless of tier
- AI post-appointment follow-up messages generated by Claude Haiku (NestJS backend only)
- Custom domain support for booking pages (Pro tier)
- Stripe billing: free (1 service, 10 bookings/mo), Pro $12/mo (unlimited services, reminders, custom domain)
- GDPR-compliant data deletion on account closure

## Out of Scope

- Payment collection at booking (pricing displayed only — payments handled outside the platform)
- Video conferencing integrations (Zoom, Google Meet links)
- Group bookings or multi-attendee events
- Native mobile apps (Capacitor shell is web-only PWA)
- SMS marketing or bulk messaging outside of transactional reminders
- AI scheduling suggestions or automatic rescheduling
- Multi-provider teams or organization accounts

## Immutable Constraints

1. **Calendar OAuth tokens are always encrypted at rest in SQLite.** Plaintext tokens must never be stored. Encryption logic lives in `api/src/auth/`. Agents must not weaken or bypass encryption.
2. **Twilio credentials are never in the frontend.** All Twilio API calls are made exclusively from `api/src/reminders/`. Agents must not expose Twilio keys to the client.
3. **Reminder opt-out must always work** — regardless of subscription tier, billing status, or any other condition. No gate or feature flag may disable opt-out.
4. **Pro pricing is hardcoded at $12/month.** Agents must never alter Stripe pricing constants or price IDs.
5. **Max buffer time is 4 hours — hardcoded.** Agents must not change this limit in any validation, config, or UI.
6. **GDPR deletion is mandatory on account close.** All provider data, client booking data, and calendar tokens must be deleted within 30 days of account closure request.
7. **Claude API is called exclusively from NestJS backend** — never from the frontend or any Capacitor plugin.
