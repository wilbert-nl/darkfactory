# MISSION.md — date-match

## What This Builds

A relationship compatibility app where partners complete structured questionnaires (values, attachment style), receive a scored compatibility breakdown by category, and access AI-generated conversation starters and daily relationship prompts. Couples link accounts via invite to unlock shared features.

## Primary Users

- Couples (established or newly forming) who want structured insight into their compatibility and guidance on bridging differences
- Individuals preparing for or reflecting on relationships

## In Scope

- Onboarding questionnaire covering values, attachment style, love languages, conflict style
- Compatibility score with category-level breakdown (communication, values alignment, conflict resolution, etc.)
- AI conversation starters generated by Claude Haiku for identified gap areas
- Relationship guidelines and best-practice content (static + AI-generated)
- Daily prompts for couples (Pro)
- Couples mode: link two accounts via expiring invite link (24h), sync questionnaire data server-side
- Local-first storage with backend sync for couples linking
- Free tier: basic score visible, no AI coaching, no couples mode
- Pro tier ($6.99/mo via Stripe): full AI coaching, couples mode, daily prompts
- GDPR-compliant full user data deletion (cascading)

## Out of Scope

- Matching or recommendation engine connecting strangers (this is not a dating app)
- Chat or messaging between users
- Group or multi-couple features
- Professional therapy services or licensed counseling
- Social profiles, discovery feeds, or public content
- In-app video or audio calls

## Immutable Constraints

1. Questionnaire responses are sensitive PII — they must never appear in logs, error messages, API error bodies, or stack traces.
2. Couples mode invite links expire in exactly 24 hours — no extension mechanism.
3. This app is not a substitute for professional therapy — a disclaimer to that effect must appear in the AI coaching UI; agents must never remove or hide it.
4. User data deletion must cascade completely across all tables and storage — no orphaned PII after account deletion (GDPR Article 17).
5. No matching or recommendation engine may be built — the app connects partners, not strangers.
6. AI coaching responses must not be stored longer than the session; raw Claude API responses are never persisted to the database.
7. Pro pricing ($6.99/mo) is hardcoded — agents must never change it.
