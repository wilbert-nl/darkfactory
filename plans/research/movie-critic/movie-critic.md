# MovieCritic — Research Brief

## What It Is
MovieCritic is a two-sided marketplace connecting indie filmmakers with vetted professional critics for pre-release screenings and paid reviews. Filmmakers get credible early coverage; critics get a new revenue stream outside editorial budgets.

## Competitors
| Name | Description |
|------|-------------|
| FilmFreeway | Festival submission platform, 12% commission, 100K+ films — not a critic marketplace |
| IndyRed | #1 indie film review site, editorial model only, not hire-a-critic |
| Film Threat / Way Too Indie / IndieWire | Established editorial outlets, not for hire, require press outreach |
| Rotten Tomatoes | Aggregator, requires recognized publication affiliation; no direct booking |
| Fiverr | General gig marketplace with some review gigs, unvetted, low credibility |

No direct competitor for vetted paid pre-release critic booking exists — this is a genuine gap in the market.

## Market Size
50,000+ indie films submitted to festivals annually via FilmFreeway alone. If 5% seek paid pre-release reviews, that is 2,500 potential buyer transactions per year at launch, scaling as indie distribution restructures (IndieWire reports weakening of traditional gatekeepers in 2026). Indie film marketing budgets range $1K–10K; a $50–250 review is accessible spend. The 2026 landscape of fewer traditional gatekeepers increases demand for credible third-party voices.

## MVP Features
1. Critic profile pages — bio, sample reviews, genre specializations, publication history
2. Filmmaker brief form — genre, runtime, target audience, Vimeo screener link
3. Booking and payment system — filmmaker pays upfront, funds held in escrow
4. Secure screener delivery — password-protected Vimeo Review or Shift72 link
5. Published review on platform + private feedback report to filmmaker
6. Critic vetting process — publication history verification before listing

## Differentiators
1. Verified critics only — publication history required; not a Fiverr-style gig marketplace, ensuring credibility for filmmakers and third-party legitimacy
2. Structured review format — standardized scoring on story, performance, cinematography, and marketability; actionable rather than opinionated prose only
3. Distribution intelligence add-on — critic recommends relevant festivals, streaming platforms, and distributors based on genre and budget; high-value upsell

## Profitability
**Model:** Marketplace commission 20–30% per review booking + optional critic pro listing fee
**Estimate:** 100 reviews/mo × $150 avg × 25% commission = $3,750/mo ($45K ARR). At 500 reviews/mo = $18,750/mo ($225K ARR). Critic pro listing at $20/mo adds predictable recurring revenue on top.

## Build Ease: 4/5
Standard two-sided marketplace architecture. AI accelerates critic-to-filmmaker matching by genre and style, automated critique quality scoring, and draft review scaffolding for critics. The hard part is supply-side: acquiring and manually vetting the first 50–100 credible critics before the platform has enough filmmakers to attract them organically.

## Legal Risks
- FTC pay-for-review disclosure — platform must display clear paid-review disclosure on every published review or faces regulatory action; this is non-negotiable
- Defamation risk — harsh or negative reviews can lead to legal threats from filmmakers; editorial independence clauses and terms of service must be drafted by a lawyer
- Screener piracy — source files must use DRM or visible watermarking (Vimeo Review or Shift72); platform is liable if screener leaks via its delivery mechanism
- Fraudulent critic credentials — background checks or editorial board vetting process required; misrepresented credentials expose platform to reputational and legal risk
