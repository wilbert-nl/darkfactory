# App Ideas Backlog

## Lifestyle & Social

### 1. GiftChecker
Track gifts given and received across events and people. Prevents duplicate gifts at weddings, birthdays, and reunions. Friends and family share a wishlist or a "already received" registry.

### 2. DateMatch
Relationship compatibility matching via questionnaires and behavioral prompts. Goes beyond swiping — surfaces compatibility scores, conversation starters, and relationship guidelines per match.

### 3. ChefConnect
Marketplace connecting home chefs with food lovers. Chefs list their meals, workers/delivery people fulfill orders. Think Airbnb for homemade food.

### 4. CrowdShot
Crowdsource real-time photos and videos of any location. Request a shot from someone currently there — useful for travelers, event scouts, and real estate.

---

## Marketplaces

### 5. Rentals
Peer-to-peer rental marketplace for items, equipment, and short-term properties. Owners list what they have; renters browse by location and duration.

### 6. WeBuy
Classifieds buy-and-sell platform. Local and national listings with condition ratings, price negotiation, and escrow-style safe checkout.

### 7. LandMatch
Connect land sellers with land seekers. Specialized property marketplace with lot/hectare filters, location maps, and document verification support.

### 8. TravelConnect
Connect travel agencies with individual travelers. Agencies post curated packages; travelers post their dream itinerary and get matched with agencies.

---

## Productivity & Tools

### 9. ProConAid
Decision-making assistant with weighted pros/cons scoring. Users list options, assign weights to criteria, and get a ranked recommendation. Shareable decision reports.

### 10. CalendR *(STARTED)*
Appointment scheduler for small businesses. Booking page, calendar sync, reminders, and client management.

### 11. Online Organizer
Digital life planner combining notes, tasks, habits, and a daily planner — inspired by physical planners like Starbucks planners. Aesthetic, printable layouts.

### 12. LocalFirst
A suite of privacy-first utility apps where all data stays on the device. Premium tier adds encrypted cloud backup and multi-device sync. No account required for free tier.

## Health & Professional

### 13. ScribeSpeak *(STARTED)*
Record audio → automatic transcription to formatted text. Designed for doctors, nurses, lawyers, and journalists who need fast voice-to-document workflows.

### 14. TrackPx *(STARTED)*
Patient records tracker for healthcare workers. Lightweight EHR alternative for clinics without full hospital systems. Offline-capable.

## Entertainment & Media

### 15. SongVote
YouTube-integrated voting system for live song requests at events, bars, and streams. Audience votes; the most-voted song queues next. DJ/host dashboard.

### 16. MovieCritic
Pre-release movie critic marketplace. Indie filmmakers hire vetted critics to screen and review before launch. Saves them from bad opening weekends.

### 17. AnimeWedding
AI-powered converter that turns wedding videos into anime-style animated videos. Shareable reels, customizable art styles.

### 18. CommentsReader
YouTube comments text-to-speech player. Browse and listen to comments hands-free while doing something else.

### 19. CommentStats
YouTube comments analytics dashboard for creators. Sentiment analysis, top keywords, audience demographics from comment patterns, engagement trends.

## AI & Creative

### 20. FitMe
Virtual try-on tool. Upload a selfie and a clothing image → AI outputs a photo of you wearing that item. For online shoppers and fashion brands.

### 21. WarpSelfie
Place yourself in any location via AI. Upload your photo + a place photo → AI composites a realistic image of you there.

### 22. AudioSwap
Quick tool to combine or swap audio tracks with YouTube videos. Replace background music, dub voiceovers, or sync a different audio to a video clip.

## Finance & Gamification

### 23. LotteryApp
Online lottery platform. Participants pool money; winner takes 95%, platform keeps 5%. Transparent draw mechanism, verifiable fairness.

### 24. PointSystem
Gamified point-earning platform for any website or community. Users earn points for activities (limited per period), redeem for rewards or status.

## Utility

### 25. Alalarm *(STARTED)*
Recurring alarm app that repeats on custom intervals, not just daily. For medication reminders, exercises, water breaks, and focus timers.

### 26. NearAlert
Location-based proximity alarm. Set a destination; phone alerts you when you're near. Built for travelers, commuters, and people who fall asleep on transit.

### 27. CompareTable
Multi-item comparison tool. Add items, define criteria, score each — outputs a sortable, filterable comparison table. Use cases: diet plans, gadgets, job offers.

## Niche

### 28. SyntheticSummer
Website/experience platform for people in cold countries to virtually experience summer — beach ambience, heat simulators, curated tropical content, wellness plans.

### 29. CF4Autofill
Automated form-filling tool specifically for CF4 (Philippine customs clearance forms). Saves profiles, auto-populates repeated fields, export-ready.
